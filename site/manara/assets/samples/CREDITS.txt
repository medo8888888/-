====================================================

All images come from Wikimedia Commons. Licences were checked with the Commons API
(imageinfo / extmetadata: LicenseShortName, Artist, Credit) on 2026-10-07.
Modifications for every image: downscaled (longest side <= 640 px) and recompressed as JPEG;
samples-data.js holds the same images downscaled again to 320 px wide (data URIs).
Public-domain and CC0 images need no attribution; it is given anyway.
