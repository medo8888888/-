Who We Are, and Where We Work

Welcome to the “Takamul” Family

Takamul Association for Building Values and Development is an independent, non-profit civil society organization, registered in accordance with the law, that seeks to build people, strengthen the system of ethical and social values, and support sustainable development through distinctive and innovative projects.

We are delighted to have you join us as an active member contributing to the journey of development and building. Your presence adds real value to our efforts to entrench ethical values, support economic and social empowerment, and achieve sustainable development.

  • Integration and ConnectionEffective coordination among civil society associations to build fruitful partnerships and use resources efficiently.
  • Empowerment and ContinuityMoving from direct relief to vocational qualification and sustainable productive empowerment.
  • Dual ImpactA balanced combination of values building (Yanabee) and economic and social empowerment (Manafea).
  • Self-SustainabilityInvestment-based and social operations that ensure stable resources to fund programs.

Administrative and Strategic Answer Guide

Frequently Asked Questions

Key Questions Founders and the Board of Directors Need to Address

Administrative and Strategic Answer Guide
Q1Why does this institution exist now? What is the core problem we seek to solve, and what unique value do we offer?
Administrative Response

The institution exists to address a real development and social gap, namely the fragmentation of efforts and the lack of integration between Turkish civil society organizations and diaspora community associations, alongside the pressing need for a model that combines values-based development with sustainable economic qualification.

Our added value lies in achieving “Dual Impact” through the “Yanabee” initiative for educational and values building and the “Manafea” initiative for economic empowerment and employment, while serving as a comprehensive coordinating platform that provides resources and prevents duplicate initiatives.

Q2What is our long-term vision (5–10 years)? And how do we define “success”?
Administrative Response

Strategic vision (2035): Achieving global leadership in building values and empowering communities in humanitarian and developmental terms.

Concept of success: Measured through comprehensive key performance indicators (KPIs):

Social and Values ImpactRaising generations with strong cohesion in values and upbringing.
Economic ImpactTransforming beneficiaries from aid recipients into independent productive forces.
Geographic ExpansionImplementing the approved phased plan from Istanbul to the Turkish provinces, the Islamic countries and the world.
Self-SustainabilityFunding at least 50% of the operating budget through economic enterprises and social investment.
Q3What core values (Core Values) and ethics will govern the work culture?
Administrative Response

The institution’s culture rests on five ethical and institutional pillars:

  • Integration and connection (Synergy): Cooperation and preventing duplication of effort.
  • Empowerment and continuity (Empowerment): Focusing on building people rather than temporary care.
  • Transparency and governance (Transparency): Full financial and administrative disclosure.
  • Social innovation (Innovation): Providing flexible and modern development solutions.
  • Responsibility and moderation (Accountability): Commitment to the highest standards of professional and social integrity.
Q4What legal entity and legislative frameworks are best suited to our activity?
Administrative Response

The entity is an independent, non-profit development civil society association, registered under the provisions of the Turkish Law on Associations in Istanbul. Its bylaws include the authority to establish economic, commercial and investment enterprises and to open branches and representative offices inside and outside Türkiye, providing legal cover for all field and investment activities.
