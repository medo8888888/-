Main Development Initiatives and Areas of Work

Two Development Initiatives and a Supporting Track

The Association achieves its field and educational objectives through two main development initiatives, supported by a track for institutional coordination and integration.

3.1 The “Yanabee” Initiative – Intellectual and Values Building and Interactive Education

Focus area: values education, intellectual building, cultural and social communication, and adaptation.

3.2 The “Manafea” Initiative – Solidarity, Mutual Support and Economic Empowerment

Focus area: addressing economic challenges, economic empowerment, employment, and solidarity and mutual support.

Membership

Takamul Association for Building Values and Development is an independent, non-profit civil society organization, registered in accordance with the law, that seeks to build people, strengthen the system of ethical and social values, and support sustainable development through distinctive and innovative projects.
