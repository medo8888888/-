# MANARA — Qatar context (verified October 2026)

For the page builders, the report and pitch writers, and the student who must defend the project
in front of Qatari judges. It answers four questions:

1. Which Qatar facts and figures may we show? (§1)
2. Which Qatari systems must MANARA plug into, not replace, and what gap is left? (§2)
3. How is emergency response in Qatar really structured, so the *dispatch* module is honest? (§2b)
4. Where does MANARA sit among Qatari student competitions, what are the risks, and what do we
   answer to ten Qatar-specific judge questions? (§3–§5)

**Conventions.** `S18…S74` are rows in `docs/MANARA-SOURCES.md` — the only place a number may come
from; show the short source next to it. **secondary** = a news page reporting an official
statement (the official page could not be read). **N/A** = we looked and could not verify; show
"N/A", never a guess. Arabic is drafted for a Gulf audience; official Arabic names of bodies and
systems (e.g. the warning system) must be copied from the official site before anything is printed.
Never use a Qatar number that is not in the table below or in the sources file.

## 2b. How emergency response in Qatar is structured (for the dispatch module)

**What is documented.** 999 is the Ministry of Interior's emergency number, run by its Central
Operations Department (S27, S38). Fires and rescue → Civil Defence (reports by 999 or Metrash).
Medical calls → HMC's Ambulance Service and its emergency medical dispatchers, who send an
ambulance and take the patient to "the appropriate emergency department" (S72). Police, traffic
and security → the Ministry of Interior. Who takes which incident type is public knowledge but
the exact internal split is not published on a page we could read: **confirm with an official
contact before stating it as a rule**.

| Unit type in MANARA | Real Qatari body | Verified | Not verified — do not claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fire / rescue | Qatar Civil Defence | reports via 999/Metrash; actual 7–10 min (2017); drones (S26, S39) | station counts (20 in 2017 vs 23 elsewhere), positions, response *targets* |
| Ambulance | HMC Ambulance Service | target 75 % in 10/15 min; 2023 actuals 93/95 % urgent calls; >200,000 emergency calls a year (S24) | live vehicle positions (needs the authority's CAD), number of units |
| Police | Ministry of Interior | 999 is the MoI's number | response times, post locations |
| Hospital | HMC Emergency Departments: Hamad General (Trauma and Emergency Center), Al Wakra, The Cuban Hospital, Hazm Mebaireek General, Aisha Bint Hamad Al Attiyah, Women's Wellness and Research Center; Paediatric Emergency Centers: Al Sadd, Airport, Al Rayyan, Al Khor, Aisha Bint Hamad Al Attiyah, Al Wakra (S72); Hamad Trauma Center = Level I (S73) | list and capability words above | coordinates, bed or ED capacity, "only trauma centre", other providers' capabilities |

**Design rules that follow (put these in `sim.js` / `mission.js`):**

1. **Rank by minutes, not kilometres.** Qatar's own targets are in minutes (S24, S26). ETA =
   Dijkstra over road segments with `length / (free-flow speed × congestion(segment, time))`; SIM
   congestion; closed roads removed. A real example of why: after the Oct 2018 rain, several
   tunnels were flooded (S28) — closed roads change which station is fastest.
2. **Capability flags on hospitals** (a patient goes to the nearest hospital that can take them):
   `ed24` (emergency department, 24 h), `paedED` (paediatric), `trauma1` (Level I), `obstetric`
   (Women's Wellness and Research Center). Capacity is a **labelled SIM assumption**, not data.
3. **Demo map is fictional.** Stations, posts and hospitals are invented points on the Doha-style
   map; the real hospital names may appear only as capability *types* ("trauma-capable
   hospital"), never with invented coordinates or travel times. Every ETA says SIM.
4. **999 stays the dispatcher.** The dispatch card says "recommended" until the (simulated)
   control room approves; states: recommended → approved → dispatched → en route → on scene →
   cleared, each timestamped. Real integration = a pre-filled package into the authority's CAD;
   needs an agreement.
5. **A verified package is a courtesy to a busy control room** (S27: in 2014 only about one 999
   call in ten was a genuine emergency). We do not claim MANARA reduces false calls — we have not
   measured it.
6. **Optional online "nearby facilities (informational)" on the phone page**: with consent, use
   the phone's location once, never store or send it anywhere except the lookup; query
   OpenStreetMap for `amenity=hospital`, `amenity=police`, `amenity=fire_station` (check tags on
   the OSM wiki before shipping); show **distance, not ETA**, plus "call 999". OSM completeness
   in Qatar is unknown: say "may be incomplete". Offline: a clear message, no map.
7. **Demo scenarios must include a "farther but faster" case** (spec), e.g. a farther station
   whose road is clear beats a nearer one whose road is jammed or flooded, with the runner-up
   and both distances shown.

### 3.2 How MANARA is different (say it politely)

| | ISEF 2025 H2S drone project (as publicly reported) | MANARA |
|---|---|---|
| Hazards | hydrogen sulfide | six on one pipeline: fire and smoke, gas (LPG, H2S, CO), flash flood, dust storm, extreme heat, someone needs help |
| Core idea | a drone detects the leak; geofence for workers | the **fixed sentinel** is the product; the drone is an optional concept for an authority |
| Who it serves | workers near a leak | every person near *any* danger: woken, told what to do for *this* hazard in their language and format, routed, counted |
| Safeguard against false alarms | not public | two independent sensor keys plus a human approval ("proof before panic") |
| After detection | not public | hazard-aware route, "I'm safe / I need help" count, hand-off card and CAP export, nearest-fastest responder recommendation |

> MANARA is the last-100-metres platform *around* detection systems like the H2S drone — not a
> competitor to them. If a drone found a leak, MANARA would be what tells people which way to walk.

**One-line positioning (AR / EN):** «الصقور تكافح النار… والمنارة تُخرج الجميع بأمان» /
"The falcons fight the fire. The lighthouse gets everyone out." Never "first", "unique" or "saves lives".

## 4. Risks and how to handle them

| Risk | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Copying the national alert tone or screen | The MoI warned of legal consequences for playing or circulating the national warning sounds (S36) | Own sound; EXERCISE banner on every demo alert; never record or reuse the official tone |
| Drones | Law No. 10 of 2026: QCAA licensing, registration, penalties up to seven years and QAR 300,000 for some offences (S34); executive regulations were still to come | The drone is a **concept for Civil Defence**. No student flight near people or buildings. Any video clip: authorised operator, permit checked with QCAA first, no payload drop |
| "Replacing" 999, Civil Defence or the national warning | They are the authorities | Every page says "plugs into". 999 stays the dispatcher |
| Sensitive events | Kuwait 2024 fire, the 2014 and 2016 Doha incidents, regional events in 2026 | Official facts only, respectful wording, no imagery; never attack or war framing; do not mention the 2026 regional alerts at all |
| Cheap-sensor limits | MQ sensors are cross-sensitive, drift, and need **over 48 hours** burn-in per the datasheets (S60–S63) — a 3-minute booth warm-up gives indicative readings only | Say it on the booth; readings are "indicative"; the demo uses safe stand-ins (hand-sanitiser vapour, a hot mug, a hair dryer, a cup of water) |

## 5. Ten Qatar-specific judge questions, with answers

Keep every answer under 40 seconds. Numbers come only from the sources file.

**1. «أليس لدى قطر نظام تحذير وطني يصل إلى كل الهواتف؟» — Doesn't Qatar already have a national warning that reaches every phone?**
- **AR:** بلى. يرسل نظام التحذير العام التابع لوزارة الداخلية تنبيهات بالعربية والإنجليزية مع اهتزاز ونغمة، إلى الجميع أو إلى مناطق محددة (المصدر: الداخلية). ولهذا لا تحاول منارة استبداله. الرسالة العامة لا تعرف مَن النائم في الغرفة 203، ومَن الأصمّ، وأيّ مخرج مغلق الآن، ومَن لم يُحصَ بعد. هذا هو «آخر 100 متر» الذي نعمل عليه، ثم نسلّم الجهات حزمة موثّقة. ولا نقلّد نغمة الإنذار الوطني، فقد حذّرت الوزارة من إساءة استخدامها.
- **EN:** Yes. The Ministry of Interior's warning system sends Arabic and English alerts with vibration and a ringtone to everyone or to chosen areas (S35). That is why MANARA does not replace it. A message to every phone cannot know who is asleep in room 203, who is Deaf, which exit is locked right now, or who has not been counted. That last 100 metres is our job, and we hand the authorities a verified package. We also do not copy the national alert sound — the Ministry warned about that (S36).

**2. «إنذارات المباني تصل أصلًا إلى غرفة عمليات الدفاع المدني، فما الجديد؟» — Building alarms already reach the Civil Defence operations room. What is new?**
- **AR:** صحيح: نظام المراقبة المركزي لإنذارات المباني يرفع الإنذار إلى غرفة عمليات الداخلية والدفاع المدني (المصدر: صحيفة ذا بينينسولا، 2013)، والقرار الوزاري 18 لسنة 2014 يُلزم سكن العمال بمكشفات دخان ونظام إنذار. هذا كله يُخبر غرفة العمليات. منارة تُخبر كل شخص بما يفعل بلغته، وتوقظ مَن لا يسمع، وتحصي مَن خرج. ولا نحلّ محل لوحات الإنذار المعتمدة؛ منارة مكمِّلة، وأي ربط بنظام حقيقي يحتاج موافقة الجهة.
- **EN:** True: building alarm boards feed the MoI and Civil Defence operations room (S38), and Ministerial Decision 18 of 2014 requires smoke detectors and an alarm in workers' accommodation (S33). Those tell the control room. MANARA tells each person what to do in their own language, wakes people who cannot hear a siren, and counts who got out. We do not replace certified alarm panels; connecting to any real system needs the authority's approval.

**3. «ماذا عن الطائرة المسيّرة؟ القانون الجديد صارم.» — What about the drone? The new law is strict.**
- **AR:** القانون رقم 10 لسنة 2026 يضع الترخيص والتسجيل تحت الهيئة العامة للطيران المدني، وبعض المخالفات عقوبتها حتى 7 سنوات وغرامة 300,000 ريال (المصدر: تغطية صحفية للقانون). لذلك المسيّرة في منارة **مفهوم** تشغّله جهة مرخّصة مثل الدفاع المدني — الذي يملك أصلًا مسيّرات إطفاء — وليست جزءًا لازمًا: المستشعر الثابت يعمل بدونها. وعرضنا لا يحلّق فوق أحد.
- **EN:** Law No. 10 of 2026 puts licensing and registration under the Civil Aviation Authority, with some offences carrying up to seven years and QAR 300,000 (S34). So in MANARA the drone is a concept for a licensed agency such as Civil Defence — which already has firefighting drones (S39) — and it is optional: the fixed sentinel works without it. Our demo flies over nobody.

**4. «كيف يختلف مشروعكم عن مشروع المسيّرة التي كشفت كبريتيد الهيدروجين في آيسف 2025؟» — How is MANARA different from the 2025 ISEF drone H2S project?**
- **AR:** نحترم ذلك الفريق؛ المعلن عن مشروعه هو عنوانه وجائزته فقط: مسيّرة تكشف تسرّب H2S مع سياج جغرافي لسلامة العمال (S43)، ولم نطّلع على تصميمه فلا نصفه. منارة ليست مشروع مسيّرة: المنتج هو المستشعر الثابت، وتغطي ستة أخطار على مسار واحد، وما نقدمه هو السلسلة المتمحورة حول الإنسان: تحقق، إيقاظ مستهدف، مسار صحيح لكل خطر، إحصاء، تسليم. لو كشفت مسيّرة تسرّبًا، فمنارة هي التي تخبر الناس إلى أين يمشون.
- **EN:** We respect that team. What is public about it is the title and the award: a drone-enabled H2S leak detector with geofencing for worker safety (S43); we have not seen the design, so we do not describe it. MANARA is not a drone project — the fixed sentinel is the product, one pipeline covers six hazards, and what we offer is the person-centred chain: proof, targeted waking, a hazard-correct route, a headcount and a hand-off. If a drone found a leak, MANARA is what tells people which way to walk.

**5. «هل كاشف الحريق «ذكاء اصطناعي»؟» — Is your detector "AI"?**
- **AR:** لا. هو رؤية حاسوبية بقواعد: نماذج لون وحركة وتذبذب ومراحل تحقق. دقته على صور اختبار لم يرها أثناء الضبط 66.7% فقط ونقول ذلك، ويرفض كاشف الفيديو الأجسام الحمراء الثابتة. وهو لا يقرر وحده: لا بد من مفتاحين مستقلين ثم موافقة بشرية قبل أي تنبيه عام.
- **EN:** No. It is rule-based computer vision: colour models, motion, flicker and verification layers. Its accuracy on held-out still images is 66.7 % and we say so; the video detector rejects static red objects. And it never decides alone: two independent keys, then a human approval, before any public alert.

**6. «مَن يقرر إرسال التنبيه؟ وماذا عن الإنذارات الكاذبة؟» — Who decides to alert? What about false alarms?**
- **AR:** القاعدة «الدليل قبل الذعر»: مستشعر واحد لا يتجاوز مستوى «اشتباه»؛ يلزم مفتاحان مستقلان ثم ضغطة موافقة من المشغّل. ويبقى الرقم 999 هو المرسِل. في 2014 قال مسؤول في الداخلية إن نحو واحد من كل عشرة اتصالات بـ 999 فقط كان طارئًا حقيقيًا (S27)؛ لذلك صمّمنا حزمة موثّقة لتخفّف على غرفة العمليات، لكننا لم نقِس بعدُ أثرها فلا ندّعيه.
- **EN:** The rule is "proof before panic": one sensor can never go beyond SUSPECT; it takes two independent keys and then an operator's approval, and 999 stays the dispatcher. In 2014 an MoI official said only about one in ten 999 calls was a genuine emergency (S27), so we designed a verified package to help the control room — but we have not measured its effect and do not claim one.

**7. «في قطر منع للعمل وقت الظهيرة وحدّ WBGT. ماذا تضيفون؟» — Qatar already bans midday work and sets a WBGT limit. What do you add?**
- **AR:** المنع جدول زمني، وحدّ 32.1 °م يتطلب **قياسًا** في موقع العمل (S18، S19). منارة تحوّل القياس إلى رسالة شخصية «توقف واذهب إلى الظل» بلغة العامل، وتحصي مَن وصل إلى مكان التبريد. ووجدت دراسة منظمة العمل الدولية أن العمال الذين يستطيعون تنظيم وتيرتهم وأخذ الاستراحة كانوا أقل عرضة للإجهاد الحراري (S20)، فنصمّم تذكيرًا لا إجبارًا. وقيمة WBGT من مستشعر حرارة ورطوبة **تقدير** نكتبه على الشاشة؛ الرقم القانوني يحتاج مقياسًا بكرة سوداء.
- **EN:** The ban is a schedule, and the 32.1 °C limit needs a **measurement** at the workplace (S18, S19). MANARA turns a measurement into a personal "stop and go to shade" message in the worker's language and counts who reached the cooling shelter. The ILO study found workers who could self-pace and rest had lower heat strain (S20), so we design a nudge, not enforcement. A WBGT from a temperature/humidity sensor is an **estimate**, and the screen says so; the legal figure needs a black-globe thermometer.

**8. «سكن العمال ملزَم بإنذارات أصلًا، فلماذا منارة؟» — Workers' accommodation must already have alarms. Why MANARA?**
- **AR:** في تعداد 2020 سُجّل 46.6% من السكان في مساكن عمال جماعية (S22)، والقانون يلزمها بإنذارات وخطة إخلاء (S33). الإنذار يرنّ؛ لكنه لا يقول لنائم يقرأ المالايالامية أي درج يستخدم، ولا يوقظ الأصمّ، ولا يعدّ مَن خرج. لذلك بُني توأم المبنى حول هذه الفجوة. لا نقول إننا ننقذ أرواحًا؛ نعرض محاكاة موسومة SIM تفحص الآلية فقط.
- **EN:** In the 2020 census 46.6 % of residents were counted in shared worker accommodation (S22), and the law requires alarms and an evacuation plan (S33). An alarm rings; it does not tell a sleeper who reads Malayalam which stair to use, does not wake a Deaf resident, and does not count who got out. The building twin is built around that gap. We do not say we save lives; we show a simulation labelled SIM that checks the mechanism only.

**9. «هل ادعاء «الأسرع» حقيقي وأنتم تحاكون المرور؟» — Is "fastest responder" real when your traffic is simulated?**
- **AR:** المرور في العرض **محاكاة** ونكتب SIM عليه. في الواقع يلزم مزوّد مرور حيّ وبيانات مواقع المركبات من الجهة نفسها، وهذا يحتاج اتفاقًا. اخترنا الترتيب بالدقائق لأن أهداف قطر نفسها بالدقائق: هدف الإسعاف الوصول إلى 75% من البلاغات خلال 10 دقائق في المدن (S24). وبعد أمطار أكتوبر 2018 غمرت المياه أنفاقًا (S28)؛ إغلاق طريق يغيّر أي جهة هي الأسرع. ويبقى 999 هو المرسِل.
- **EN:** The traffic in our demo is a **simulation**, labelled SIM. A real system needs a live traffic provider and the authority's own vehicle-location data, which takes an agreement. We rank by minutes because Qatar's own targets are in minutes — the ambulance target is to reach 75 % of calls within 10 minutes in urban areas (S24). After the October 2018 rain several tunnels flooded (S28); a closed road changes which unit is fastest. 999 stays the dispatcher.

**10. «هل ترجماتكم موثوقة؟ ولماذا هذه اللغات؟» — Are your translations reliable? Why these languages?**
- **AR:** العربية والإنجليزية كاملتان. اللغات الست (المالايالامية، النيبالية، البنغالية، الأردية، التاغالوغية، الهندية) **مسودات** مكتوب عليها «تحتاج مراجعة متحدث أصلي» حتى يوقّع عليها مراجع، ونسجّل ذلك في التقرير. اخترناها لأن أكبر الجاليات بحسب تقديرات السفارات 2015–2017 من الهند ونيبال وبنغلاديش والفلبين وباكستان (S23)؛ وجالية سريلانكا (سنهالية وتاميلية) خارج المجموعة حاليًا ونذكر ذلك. البطاقات المصورة تحمل الرسالة الأساسية للجميع، ولا نستخدم ترجمة آلية حيّة أبدًا.
- **EN:** Arabic and English are complete. The six community languages (Malayalam, Nepali, Bengali, Urdu, Tagalog, Hindi) are **drafts** marked "needs native-speaker review" until a reviewer signs off, and the report logs this. We chose them because the largest communities in the 2015–2017 embassy estimates come from India, Nepal, Bangladesh, the Philippines and Pakistan (S23); the Sri Lankan community (Sinhala, Tamil) is not yet covered and we say so. Picture cards carry the core message for everyone, and we never use live machine translation.
