## Context (facts, not statistics)

- **2024 Gulf residential fire (Mangaf, Kuwait):** 12 June 2024 at about 04:00, a six-storey
  building housing migrant workers; 50 people died, most from smoke inhalation while asleep;
  a locked rooftop door blocked escape; victims were mostly Indian and Filipino workers.
  Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Mangaf_building_fire ;
  https://abcnews.go.com/International/wireStory/35-people-died-building-fire-kuwait-state-news-111047163 .
  Use respectfully, no imagery; public pages say «حريق سكني في الخليج عام 2024» / "a 2024 Gulf residential fire".
- **UAE Hassantuk** connects more than 150,000 buildings to a central alarm centre that
  dispatches civil defence (Gulf News) — MANARA plugs into such systems, it does not replace them.
- **UAE drone rules (recreational):** registration, line of sight, daytime, ≤ 400 ft, no drop or
  release devices (GCAA / uavcoach summary). Saudi GACA: drones ≥ 250 g must be registered.
- **Dubai Shaheen** (1,200 L tank, used at the Al Barsha fire, Sept 2025), **Abu Dhabi Suhail**
  (jet-powered, 2025) — the other "falcons".

## Do NOT use (unverified or misleading)

"17 minutes to escape 40 years ago vs 3 minutes today"; "smoke causes 60–80% of fire deaths";
CAMS "1,940 megatonnes"; any recent Saudi national fire total; Chula Vista police drone times;
"33,510"; any night-time quiet-tone claim about UAE cell broadcast; anything "saves X lives".
