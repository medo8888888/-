## Context (facts, not statistics)

- **2024 Gulf residential fire (Mangaf, Kuwait):** 12 June 2024 at about 04:00, a six-storey
  building housing migrant workers; 50 people died, most from smoke inhalation while asleep;
  a locked rooftop door blocked escape; victims were mostly Indian and Filipino workers.
  Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Mangaf_building_fire ;
  https://abcnews.go.com/International/wireStory/35-people-died-building-fire-kuwait-state-news-111047163 .
  Use respectfully, no imagery; public pages say «حريق سكني في الخليج عام 2024» / "a 2024 Gulf residential fire".
- **UAE Hassantuk** connects more than 150,000 buildings to a central alarm centre that
  dispatches civil defence (Gulf News) — MANARA plugs into such systems, it does not replace them.
- **UAE drone rules (recreational):** registration, line of sight, daytime, ≤ 400 ft, no drop or
  release devices (GCAA / uavcoach summary). Saudi GACA: drones ≥ 250 g must be registered.
- **Dubai Shaheen** (1,200 L tank, used at the Al Barsha fire, Sept 2025), **Abu Dhabi Suhail**
  (jet-powered, 2025) — the other "falcons".

## Do NOT use (unverified or misleading)

"17 minutes to escape 40 years ago vs 3 minutes today"; "smoke causes 60–80% of fire deaths";
CAMS "1,940 megatonnes"; any recent Saudi national fire total; Chula Vista police drone times;
"33,510"; any night-time quiet-tone claim about UAE cell broadcast; anything "saves X lives".

## Qatar context (facts, not statistics)

- **Qatar Civil Defence** is part of the Ministry of Interior; the public reports emergencies by
  **999** or the **Metrash** app (QNA, 29 Jul 2026). Its Technical Requirements Guide was
  launched in 2022 (same article). 999 call handlers speak "Arabic, English, French, Chinese,
  Tagalog and Urdu" (Doha News, 2014).
- **HMC Ambulance Service** handles 999 medical calls; HMC's emergency medical dispatchers are the
  first contact for ambulance requests (Doha News, 2014). **992** is the MoI channel for Deaf
  callers (S37).
- **Mada** — Assistive Technology Center Qatar, founded in 2010 to promote digital inclusion for
  persons with disabilities (Mada website). **TASMU** — the Smart Qatar Program led by the Ministry
  of Transport and Communications, five priority sectors: healthcare, environment, sports,
  transportation, utilities/energy (MoTC). Neither is an emergency system; they are the
  accessibility and smart-solution context MANARA should respect.
- **QMD** (Qatar Meteorology Department) publishes warnings on its social-media accounts and on
  qweather.gov.qa (Marhaba / Qatar Tribune reporting).
- **Stars of Science** (Qatar Foundation): for Arab innovators aged 18–35; judges look for ideas
  that are "creative, realistic and marketable" (programme FAQ / The National). Not a school contest.
- **June 2016**: eleven people died and twelve were injured in a fire at workers' accommodation on
  the outskirts of Doha; the cause was under investigation when reported (The Peninsula,
  3 Jun 2016). Use only with official facts, respectfully, no imagery.
- **SignBridge** (Qatar Academy Doha, finalist, 2026 national exhibition): an AI system translating
  English and Arabic speech into American Sign Language (Gulf Times, 11 Jun 2026). MANARA does
  not claim sign-language translation.
